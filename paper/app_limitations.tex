\section{Limitations}
\label{app:limitations}

Ten seeds per method and task provide limited statistical power, especially
for modest differences between methods. Comparisons of conditional quality rest
on fewer episodes still, because they use delivered recommendations only, so
uncertainty intervals can be wide. Clock limits define 31\% of baseline
nondeliveries, so delivery depends on the reference machine, and
Appendix~\ref{app:outcomes} bounds the effect. The agent results are specific
to DeepSeek V4.1 Flash. We do not compare language models.
